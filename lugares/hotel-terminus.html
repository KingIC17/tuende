Hotel Terminus — Accommodation, Lobito, Benguela

Lobito's most iconic hotel, by the sea.

A seafront hotel on the Restinga do Lobito, for decades the most iconic in the province; it moved from a century-old building to a more modern one. The restaurant has a terrace and a jango facing the sea.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
