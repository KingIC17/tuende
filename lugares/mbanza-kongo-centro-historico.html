Mbanza Kongo (centro histórico) — Culture and landmarks, Centro histórico, Mbanza Kongo

Capital of the Kingdom of Kongo, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Capital of the Kingdom of Kongo from before the Portuguese arrived in 1483 until 1915. The historic centre has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2017.
