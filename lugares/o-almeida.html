O Almeida — Restaurant, Via Expressa, Luanda

Grills, seafood and Angolan dishes on the Via Expressa.

A restaurant on the Via Expressa (formerly Sem Dem) in Luanda, with grills, steaks, fish, seafood, salt cod, risottos and pizzas. The menu includes Angolan dishes such as calulu with dried and fresh fish, grilled chopa mufete and chicken cabidela. Opening hours to be confirmed.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
