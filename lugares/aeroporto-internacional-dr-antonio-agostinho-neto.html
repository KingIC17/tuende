Aeroporto Internacional Dr. António Agostinho Neto — Transport, Bom Jesus, Icolo e Bengo

All Luanda flights have used this airport since March 2026.

Luanda's airport since March 2026, about 40 km from the centre. It handles all domestic and international flights; the old 4 de Fevereiro airport closed to commercial flights. Trains run from Bungo Station.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
