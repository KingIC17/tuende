Bessangana — Club, Ilha de Luanda, Luanda

DJ parties and semba on the Ilha de Luanda.

Restaurant and event space on the Ilha de Luanda, with DJ parties, semba nights and fashion shows. In October and November 2026 it hosts DJ Jorge Caiado, the "This is House Music" party and the Roda de Semba Raiz; Angola Fashion Week 2026 took place in the Bessangana tent.
