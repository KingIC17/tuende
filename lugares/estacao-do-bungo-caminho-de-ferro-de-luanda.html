Estação do Bungo (Caminho de Ferro de Luanda) — Transport, Ingombota, Luanda

Suburban trains for 300 Kz; airport link.

The central station of the Luanda Railway. Suburban trains to Viana and Catete, the airport train and services to Dondo and Malanje leave from here.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
