Parque Nacional do Luengue-Luiana — Nature, Luiana, Cuando

A south-eastern park with its first safari camp.

A 22,610 km² national park created in 2011 in south-eastern Angola. In July 2026 Luiana Plains Safari opened, the park's first tourist camp, in the wetlands of the Cuando River, with safaris, walks and boat trips.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
