Lagoa dos Arcos — Nature, Tômbwa, Namibe

Desert lagoons with natural stone arches.

Three lagoons on the north bank of the Curoca River, about 20 km from the coast, in the desert south of Moçâmedes; also known as Arco do Carvalhão. The middle lagoon has natural sandstone arches. Access is over sand, by 4x4 only. The water depends on the rain: the lagoons were dry for six years, until 2018.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
