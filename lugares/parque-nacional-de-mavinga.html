Parque Nacional de Mavinga — Nature, Mavinga, Cuando

Rivers and savannah in the south-east, with expert guides.

A 46,076 km² national park created in 2011 next to Luengue-Luiana and managed with it. Rivers such as the Cuando and the Lomba cross it, and it forms the western edge of the Kavango-Zambezi (KAZA) conservation area. There are still landmines from the war: many park roads are in poor condition or closed, so go only with experienced guides and operators.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
