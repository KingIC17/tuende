Casa das Artes — Culture and landmarks, Talatona, Luanda

A cultural space in Talatona with an amphitheatre and an art gallery.

A cultural space in Talatona with an amphitheatre for about 300 people, an art gallery, training rooms, a bar and a shop. It hosts shows, exhibitions and arts courses.
