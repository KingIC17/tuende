Solar Pinguim — Restaurant, Uíge

Pastries and dishes with funge in central Uíge.

A restaurant and pastry shop on Rua do Comércio in Uíge city, with cakes and savoury pastries, sandwiches, quick meals, salt cod and meat dishes with funge, such as picanha and veal loin. Chicken muamba and peito alto are made to order. The map point is the street; opening hours to be confirmed.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
