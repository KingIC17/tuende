Estação do Lobito (Caminho de Ferro de Benguela) — Transport, Lobito, Benguela

Trains to Benguela, Huambo, Cuíto and Luena.

The starting station of the Benguela Railway, which runs to Luena and Luau. In June 2026 the Lobito–Luena express ran again after two months stopped by floods. There are also suburban trains between Lobito and Benguela.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
