Hotel Baía — Accommodation, Praia do Bispo, Luanda

A boutique hotel on the bay, with a rooftop pool and spa.

A four-star boutique hotel on the Bay of Luanda, part of TD Hotels. The pool and spa are on the rooftop, and the lounge bar looks out over the sunset. It has a gym, a restaurant serving local and international food and meeting rooms. It has won World Luxury Hotel Awards.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
