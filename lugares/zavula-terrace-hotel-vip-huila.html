Zavula Terrace (Hotel VIP Huíla) — Bar, Lubango, Huíla

A hotel terrace with live jazz.

The terrace of Hotel VIP Huíla, in Lubango's commercial district. In 2023 it hosted Jazz às Quintas, live music every other Thursday and one of the city's best-known gigs.
