Parque Nacional da Cangandala

O parque da palanca negra gigante, símbolo de Angola.

O mais pequeno parque nacional de Angola, com 630 km², criado para proteger a palanca negra gigante, o símbolo do país. Em agosto de 2026 foram avistadas mais de 200 palancas negras gigantes juntas. Parque Nacional da Cangandala — Nature, Cangandala, Malanje

The park of the giant sable antelope, Angola's symbol.

Angola's smallest national park, 630 km², created to protect the giant sable antelope, the country's symbol. In August 2026 more than 200 giant sable antelopes were seen together. In 2024 the government announced the park would open to visitors, but we found no confirmation that it has: check before you go.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
