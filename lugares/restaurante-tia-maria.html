Restaurante Tia Maria — Restaurant, Coqueiros, Luanda

Portuguese cooking and funge by the Coqueiros stadium.

A Portuguese restaurant in Coqueiros, next to the Coqueiros stadium and the Luanda Tennis Club. It has rustic wooden decor, meat, fish, seafood and a varied wine list, and also serves funge. The map position is approximate; opening hours to be confirmed.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
