Igreja de Nossa Senhora do Pópulo — Culture and landmarks, Centro, Benguela

A 1748 Baroque church, one of Benguela's oldest.

Built in 1748 in the Baroque style, it is one of Benguela's oldest churches. The first Mass was held in November 1748.
