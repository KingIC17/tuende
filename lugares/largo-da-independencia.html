Largo da Independência — Square and promenade, Ingombota, Luanda

Where independence was proclaimed in 1975.

The square where Agostinho Neto proclaimed Angola's independence in 1975, with his statue at the centre. Renovated, it reopened on 10 November 2025, for the 50th anniversary of independence.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
