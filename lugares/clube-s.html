Clube S — Club, Embarcadouro do Mussulo, Luanda

Concerts and parties by the Mussulo boat landing.

Concert and party venue by the Mussulo boat landing, inside the Clube Naval. It hosts Angolan and Lusophone music concerts, such as Justino Delgado in October 2026, and also runs parties at other venues. The programme and tickets are on the club's website.

Prices in Kwanza, opening hours and directions are in the Tuende app (Portuguese and English).
